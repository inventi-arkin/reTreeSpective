🌳 ReTreeSpective: Cultivating Continuous Improvement

ReTreeSpective is a real-time, highly interactive, and gamified sprint retrospective board designed to make agile team reflections engaging, visual, and fun.

Instead of traditional sticky notes on a static digital whiteboard, ReTreeSpective transforms your team's feedback into a living, breathing Sprint Garden. The health of your sprint directly influences the weather, the growth of the trees, and the overall ecosystem of your board. By turning "Went Well" and "To Improve" items into visual environmental factors, teams can instantly gauge the sentiment and success of their recent work.

✨ The Core Concept: A Living Ecosystem

At the heart of ReTreeSpective is the visual scenario. The environment reacts mathematically to the net score of your team's feedback (Went Well vs. To Improve):

☀️ Thriving Sprints: When positive feedback outweighs the negatives, the garden flourishes. The sun shines brightly, the central tree grows a massive canopy with apples, auxiliary trees sprout to form a lush forest, and flocks of birds fly across a clear anime-style meadow sky.

🌧️ Challenging Sprints: When obstacles and "To Improve" items pile up, the environment reacts. The sun sets, the sky darkens, and the wind picks up. If the sprint was particularly rough, heavy rain and lightning storms will roll in, visually representing the challenges the team faced.

🧑‍🌾 Gamified Team Avatars & The Kudos System

To encourage peer recognition and appreciation, ReTreeSpective features a robust Kudos System tied to beautifully animated, chibi-style "Gardener" avatars.

As team members receive Kudos (stars) from their colleagues, their personal Gardener avatar dynamically levels up, unlocking new gear and tools right on the board:

Novice (0-1 Stars): A humble beginner in their starting overalls.

Apprentice (2-3 Stars): Earns a classic yellow gardener's cap.

Groundskeeper (4-6 Stars): Equips thick gardening gloves and a hand-rake.

Botanist (7-11 Stars): A beautiful potted sunflower blossoms next to their avatar.

Harvester (12-19 Stars): Unlocks a metal watering can to tend to the garden.

Master Gardener (20+ Stars): Achieves ultimate recognition! The avatar dons a golden crown, golden overalls, and radiates a pulsing, magical yellow aura.

🚀 Key Features

Real-Time Collaboration: Powered by Firebase, all feedbacks, kudos, and environmental changes update instantly for everyone viewing the board.

Personalized Highlight Mode: Selecting a team member from the filter dropdown triggers an immersive "Highlight Mode." The UI shifts to a frosted-glass aesthetic, spotlighting the user's massive animated avatar, their specific feedback, and the kudos they've received, set against their personalized weather scenario.

Secure Team Management: Sprint creators and system Admins can easily manage which team members are participating in a specific sprint, ensuring the garden only shows the relevant active gardeners.

Admin Moderation: Authorized admin accounts (via domain validation @inventi.ph / @inventi.asia) can oversee the board, manage teams for any sprint, and moderate feedback to keep the environment professional.

Domain-Restricted Access: Integrated Google Authentication ensures that only authorized company personnel can access the team's private retrospectives.

🎯 Why ReTreeSpective?

Traditional retrospectives can sometimes feel repetitive or tedious. ReTreeSpective solves this by introducing visual storytelling and RPG-like progression. It encourages team members to actively participate, celebrate each other's wins to see their avatars grow, and openly discuss improvements to clear the storm clouds away.

Grow your team, weather the storms together, and harvest your successes with ReTreeSpective.
